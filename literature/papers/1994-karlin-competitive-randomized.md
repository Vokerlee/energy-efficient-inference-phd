## Findings, relevance and limits

Studies online decisions when repeated service costs can be exchanged for a one-time state change. Competitive analysis compares a causal strategy with a clairvoyant comparator under explicit adversary and cost assumptions. The work develops randomized improvements over deterministic baselines for its stated models.

Useful theoretical background for deciding when to prepare or retain an alternative numerical representation. Rent-or-buy thresholds and competitive guarantees are established ideas, not a new inference contribution. Finite argument buffers, eviction, changing costs, measured energy, quantization feasibility and downstream quality require separate models. A bound for one constrained residency phase is not a guarantee against a globally optimal accelerator schedule. No inference or hardware-energy result was reproduced.
