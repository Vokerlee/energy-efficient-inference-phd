- Evidence: First-order uncertainty-propagation framework with covariance terms.

## What it establishes

Explains propagation using sensitivity derivatives, with separate formulas
for uncorrelated and correlated inputs. It distinguishes uncertainty
propagation from propagation of a realized error.

## Relevance to our goals

A methodological reference for retaining correlations and stating the
linearization assumptions behind error diagnostics.

## Limits and transfer conditions

This is metrology guidance, not a neural-inference quality theorem. Standard
uncertainty is not the realized numerical error. Its formula does not justify
ignoring nonlinear effects, assuming quantization independence, or interpreting
local variance as a final task-quality guarantee.
