# Finding Structure with Randomness: Probabilistic Algorithms for Constructing Approximate Matrix Decompositions

- Citation key: `halko2011randomized`.
- Authors: Halko, Nathan; Martinsson, Per-Gunnar; Tropp, Joel A..
- Publication: SIAM Review, 2011.
- Source: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.4061v2); [full text reviewed](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0909.4061v2).
- Reviewed: 2026-11-08. Status: **full-text focused reading**.
- Reading focus: Range finding, orthogonalization, passes, and cost model.
- Evidence: Numerical algorithms, analysis, and experiments.

## What it establishes

Constructs approximate subspaces using randomized sampling, with explicit arithmetic/pass trade-offs.

## Relevance to our goals

A reference for measuring the cost of discovering an activation subspace.

## Limits and transfer conditions

A cheap approximation relative to full SVD may still cost more than the inference work it avoids. It does not establish a hardware-realistic online neural policy.
