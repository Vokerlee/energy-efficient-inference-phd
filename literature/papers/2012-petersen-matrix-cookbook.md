## What it establishes

Lists covariance transport under linear maps and expected quadratic forms in
terms of means and covariances. The norm reference gives spectral and
Frobenius definitions and inequalities.

## Relevance to our goals

A source for the algebra behind directional-error and mean/covariance analyses.
Use the general moment identities when independence is not assumed.

## Limits and transfer conditions

This is a reference collection, not evidence of novelty. Applying identities
to neural errors still requires a defined ensemble and transport map.
Isotropic covariance and negligible cross-layer correlations need separate
justification; the reference does not establish either for quantization.
