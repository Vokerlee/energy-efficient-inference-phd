## What it establishes

Introduces FID to compare generated and reference image feature distributions.

## Relevance to our goals

An established distributional metric for image-generation comparisons.

## Limits and transfer conditions

It is not paired-pixel agreement or a complete measure of prompt following. Feature extraction, reference statistics, preprocessing, and sample count must be controlled.
