## Findings, relevance and limits

UW-CSE-2017-03-02 describes precision autotuning for annotated C/C++ programs with application quality criteria and hardware energy models. Quality–energy exploration as a methodology is established. This is distinct from the later Inci QAPPA project despite the shared acronym. Do not infer an autonomous current-activation controller or transfer energy coefficients to another architecture. No result was reproduced here.
