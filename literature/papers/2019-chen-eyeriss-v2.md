## What it establishes

Adapts operand delivery to different bandwidth/reuse demands and processes compressed sparse weights and activations. Eyexam progressively constrains ideal throughput using workload and architectural limits.

## Relevance to our goals

A baseline for diagnosing shape, utilization, and delivery limits before adding numerical adaptation.

## Limits and transfer conditions

Compact/sparse CNN results depend on the specified models, pruning, and implementation. Network flexibility alone does not establish a universal energy advantage.
