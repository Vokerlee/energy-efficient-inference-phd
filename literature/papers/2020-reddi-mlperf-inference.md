# MLPerf Inference Benchmark

- Citation key: `reddi2020mlperf`.
- Authors: Reddi, Vijay Janapa; Cheng, Christine; Kanter, David; Mattson, Peter; Schmuelling, Guenther; Wu, Carole-Jean; others.
- Publication: ISCA, 2020.
- Source: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.02549v2); [full text reviewed](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.02549v2).
- Reviewed: 2026-11-09. Status: **full-text focused reading**.
- Reading focus: Load generation, workload scenarios, quality targets, and system boundaries.
- Evidence: Benchmark methodology and multi-system submissions.

## What it establishes

Defines inference scenarios and an external load generator, with separate accuracy and performance checks for comparable system evaluation.

## Relevance to our goals

A methodological baseline for fixing the workload and acceptance criterion before comparing execution policies.

## Limits and transfer conditions

This historical paper does not specify current submission rules. Research with pruning or changed models must declare its own protocol and cannot automatically claim MLPerf compliance.
