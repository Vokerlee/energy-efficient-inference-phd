## Findings, relevance and limits

The abstract describes a parameterized accelerator design-space framework spanning bit precision, PE types, memories, bandwidth and workloads. The record reports acceptance at a2021 MLSys workshop; this reviewed arXiv version is dated2022. It is direct precedent for quantization-aware PPA exploration, distinct from Moreau QAPPA2017. Exact dynamic-policy, activity and model-validation protocols remain unresolved from this abstract.
