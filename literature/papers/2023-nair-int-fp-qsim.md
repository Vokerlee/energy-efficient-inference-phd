## Findings, relevance and limits

The abstract describes a simulator combining existing tools for mixed integer/floating formats and quantization comparisons on LLMs and vision transformers. Merely building another flexible model-level quantization simulator is not a new research methodology. Per-MAC arithmetic, fixed matrix boundaries, causal controller state and physical-energy claims require the full protocol; do not infer their presence or absence from the abstract.
