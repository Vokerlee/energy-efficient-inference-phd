## What it establishes

Co-designs integer/floating accumulation stages for microscaling and evaluates costs at both MAC and system levels.

## Relevance to our goals

A hardware comparator for scale alignment and reduction overhead beyond multiplier precision.

## Limits and transfer conditions

A MAC-level resource breakdown is not a whole-NPU energy breakdown. Continual-learning context and implementation assumptions limit direct transfer.
