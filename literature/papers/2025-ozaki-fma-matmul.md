## Findings and relevance

Proposes FMA-based high-accuracy matrix multiplication as an alternative to more costly pair/double-word arithmetic. It is an early comparator for claiming new accurate-GEMM machinery.

## Limits

The expanded 2026 article supplies a separately reviewed error-analysis comparator. Neither numerical accuracy nor speed reported for a different reference contract automatically implies equivalence to an ordered finite-precision device model.
