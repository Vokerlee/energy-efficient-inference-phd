## Findings, relevance and limits

The publisher describes per-column-block adaptive FP4 formats and a bit-serial systolic design whose data transmission coordinates variable-latency processing elements. Format consistency is used to prevent pipeline stalls. This directly limits broad novelty claims about quantization granularity plus synchronized array execution.

The retrieved material does not establish the full assignment/calibration protocol, scheduling equations, cost model or controller state. Do not infer calibration-free online selection, a bounded M-row queue, or measured silicon energy from it. Reported speed/energy comparisons remain unverified here until full-text methods and assumptions are available.
